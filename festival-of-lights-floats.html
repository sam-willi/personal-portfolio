01The parade

The Los Altos Festival of Lights Parade has run since 1977.

A few downtown merchants started it by pulling together four or five push floats for a small parade. It is now an all-volunteer association that shuts down State and Main the Sunday after Thanksgiving, and the floats live year-round in a warehouse at the city corporation yard.

02What I do

  1. 01Construction and designSome floats are push floats. Others are fully modified vehicles with an engine, a drivetrain, and everything that comes with keeping one running.
  2. 02Sound and lightingI also do the sound and lighting engineering on the floats.
  3. 03Yearly workEvery year we do maintenance on the very old floats, refurbish what needs it, and design a brand new float from scratch.

03Clean energy

We have been converting as many floats as possible to battery power.

The old systems were not clean energy, and swapping them out means quieter floats, no exhaust in a crowd of kids, and a lot of rewiring per float.

Learning by building things.
