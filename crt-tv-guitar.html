Materials
CRT TV / Arduino / ribbon pot / birch
Role
Design, fabrication, electronics
Output
Sound + image, one signal
Status
Functioning, played live

What is it?

A CRT television, an Arduino, and a fretless ribbon neck cut from birch. Sound comes out of the TV speaker; the notes show up as bars on the screen.

01Context

I have always been drawn to analog technology and think it is too cool not to be properly appreciated.

I saw a clip from the band Electronicos Fantasticos! and was inspired to build my own CRT TV guitar.

02Signal path

  1. 01RibbonThe Arduino reads voltage off a ribbon potentiometer as my finger slides along it.
  2. 02ArduinoEach position on the ribbon maps to a frequency.
  3. 03RF modulator → TVRCA cables I soldered feed an RF modulator, so the signal becomes both audio and image.

03Result

It plays more like an otamatone than a guitar.

The ribbon pot works like a fretless neck, so I can slide between notes and add vibrato. In the video I play “In My Life” by the Beatles. I couldn’t decide what song to learn, and that’s what my grandma suggested.
