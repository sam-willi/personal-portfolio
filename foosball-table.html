What is it?

A six-person engineering capstone at Mountain View High School. We built a full-size foosball table out of plywood, 3D printed the players, and wired an Arduino scoreboard into the cabinet.

01Context

The brief was a capstone six people could build together that would still be used after we graduated.

A foosball table in a building everyone walks through every day fit. I wrote the abstract and ran the Gantt chart, then worked the build: cutting panels on the CNC router, gluing up the cabinet, staining, and assembly. When the schedule slipped a month I was the one re-planning it.

02Building it

  1. 01CADEvery panel was cut from one Onshape model.
  2. 02CabinetWe cut the panels on the CNC router, then glued up the cabinet, stained it, and assembled it.
  3. 03ScoreboardWe wired an Arduino scoreboard into the cabinet, with a seven-segment display.

03Result

It lives in the lobby of the Student Services building.

Interlocking joints meant the whole cabinet went together without a jig. The 3D printed players held up to being hit, the rods spun in the CNC-drilled holes, and the table is the right height to stand and play.

Learning by building things.
