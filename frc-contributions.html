01On the field

I did the talking at every competition.

Strategy with our alliance partners, match plans with the drive team, alliance selection, and answers for the judges. As president I ran the team through a full season, from summer training to the World Championship, and we qualified for Worlds all four years I was on the team.

02What I did on the team

  1. 01Scouting + strategyBuilt the scouting apps, ran data collection, and led the picklist meeting where we argued robots head to head. Then I took the list to the field and made the picks.
  2. 022025 end effectorDesigned the mechanism that picked up both game pieces in Onshape and took it through seven versions in five weeks. It held up through three regionals and Worlds.
  3. 03Leave Your LegacyRaised $44,000 for a new CNC router. Parents and alumni bought engraved plaques that hang in the machine shop.
  4. 04Kohl’s robotics hubI led turning a vacant Kohl’s in Mountain View into a robotics center for the team. We got the building for a dollar a month and built a full-scale FRC field on the old retail floor out of plywood and 2x4s. Then I hosted a Week Zero there for teams across the Bay Area and ran the schedule, field setup, and match logistics.
