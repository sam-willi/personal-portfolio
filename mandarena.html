What is it?

I took four years of Mandarin class. I could read and write almost fluently but I still couldn’t follow a conversation.

01The gap

Reading Mandarin and understanding it out loud turned out to be really different.

Class taught me to read and write. Nobody practiced listening with me. And every listening tool I found used random words I’d never been taught, so I couldn’t tell if I was bad at listening or just hadn’t learned the words yet. So the site builds listening practice out of your own vocabulary list: the words you have actually been taught.

02How it works

  1. 01ImportPaste in a list, type it in manually, or use the words from a textbook unit.
  2. 02GenerateThe sentences only use words from your list, plus really basic words like 我 and 的.
  3. 03CheckEach sentence gets checked for grammar and for words that aren’t on the list before anyone hears it.
  4. 04ListenYou hear the sentence first without seeing the characters, then type what it means. It gets harder as you get them right.

03Result

Teachers added it to the curriculum.

The AI kept writing sentences that were wrong, so we added a validation filter: every sentence gets checked before you see it. James checked the Mandarin as a native speaker, Yasen built the logic that adjusts difficulty, and I built the platform and put the pieces together. Now anyone at my high school can use it.
